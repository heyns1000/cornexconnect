## Google Play Store

### Short Description (80 chars max)
AI-powered manufacturing & distribution management for hardware store networks.

### Full Description (4000 chars max)
CornexConnect is the enterprise platform that connects Cornex factories, global distributors, and 3,000+ hardware stores across South Africa — all from one app.

**For Factory Managers:**
- Schedule and monitor production runs across multiple lines
- Track real-time efficiency, quality scores, and waste reduction
- Set up automation rules for inventory, production, and maintenance
- Design and print product labels with built-in printer management
- Get AI-powered insights for cost reduction and market expansion

**For Buyers & Distributors:**
- Browse and manage your network of 3,000+ hardware stores by province
- Create and track purchase orders through the full lifecycle
- Optimize sales routes with Google Maps integration
- Use the mobile field app for store visits, photo capture, and on-site ordering
- Monitor AI-driven demand forecasts and smart reorder suggestions

**For Administrators:**
- Manage users, roles, and departments with fine-grained access control
- Complete audit trail of all user actions across the platform
- Bulk data import from Excel with validation and accuracy tracking
- Business intelligence dashboards with export to CSV/PDF
- Achievement system to gamify team performance

**Key Features:**
- Multi-provider login (Google, Microsoft, GitHub, Facebook)
- Real-time dashboard with animated KPIs
- AI-powered inventory optimization and demand forecasting
- Route optimization for sales representatives
- Store visit tracking with photo documentation
- Multi-currency support (ZAR, USD, EUR, GBP)
- Dark mode support
- Offline-capable mobile field app

Built for the South African hardware distribution industry. Manage your entire supply chain from factory floor to store shelf.

## Privacy Policy Requirements

Both stores require a privacy policy URL. It must cover:

- [ ] What data you collect (email, name, company, location, device info)
- [ ] How data is used (authentication, audit logging, analytics, AI insights)
- [ ] Third-party services (Google OAuth, Microsoft OAuth, GitHub OAuth, Facebook OAuth, Google Maps)
- [ ] Data retention and deletion policy
- [ ] Contact information for privacy inquiries
- [ ] POPIA compliance (South Africa's data protection law)
- [ ] Hosted at a publicly accessible URL
